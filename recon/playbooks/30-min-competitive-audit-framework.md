# The 30-Minute Local Service Business Competitive Audit

**Source:** @theseoguy_ on X (screenshots shared by Jim 2026-05-14)
**Use case:** Quick competitive audit to identify exact gaps vs top-3 ranked competitors in your local market

---

## The Setup

1. Open a fresh browser
2. Log out of all your accounts
3. Search **your main keyword + your city**
   - Example: "personal injury lawyer dallas" or "hvac company plano"

---

## Step 1: Audit the Top 3 Google Business Profiles in the Map Pack

For EACH of the top 3, write down:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Review count (total)** | Authority signal |
| **Review count from last 90 days specifically** | Freshness — playbook elsewhere says this is #1 map pack ranking factor |
| **Primary category** | Wrong category tanks campaign |
| **Whether business name has the keyword in it** | Exact-match names get massive ranking boost |
| **Whether they have a physical address showing** | Service area businesses without visible address rank harder |
| **Average star rating** | Quality signal |

## Step 2: Click Each of Their Websites and Audit

For each top-3 competitor's website:

| Element | What to capture |
|---|---|
| **Meta title** | Does it include keyword + city? Front-loaded? |
| **H1 on homepage** | Does it match search query? |
| **URL structure** | Does it include city + service? |
| **Service pages count** | How many service-specific pages? |
| **Location pages count** | How many city/suburb pages? |

## Step 3: Backlink Analysis (Ahrefs or free checker)

For each top-3 competitor:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Domain rating** | Overall authority |
| **Referring domain count** | Total link diversity |
| **New referring domains in last 90 days** | Velocity — are they actively building? |

## Step 4: Now Do the Same Audit on YOUR Business

Same metrics across the board.

## Step 5: Spreadsheet Side-by-Side

Put the data in a table. **You will see exactly where the gap is.**

---

## 🎯 The 90% Rule (the punchline)

> "90% of the time it is one of three things:
> - They have more recent reviews than you
> - They have more local backlinks than you
> - Their meta title and h1 match the search query and yours doesn't
>
> Every single one of those is fixable."

---

## Why this framework matters

Pairs with the longer local SEO playbook in `recon/playbooks/local-seo-pi-playbook.md`. That playbook is the strategy; this is the diagnostic. Run this every 90 days to track delta as fixes deploy.

The 30-minute time-box is the genius of it — most "audits" take a week and produce 50 pages of "recommendations." This produces 3 actionable fixes per quarter, which compounds.
