## TL;DR — where leads actually come from in Google

When someone gets in a car accident and searches "personal injury lawyer near me" on their phone:

1. **Local Service Ads** at the very top
2. **Map pack** — three Google Business Profiles
3. **Organic website rankings**

**60% of leads go to the map pack.** That is where the money is. Focus there first.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile

### Verified physical address
- NOT a PO box. NOT a virtual office.
- Real office you can verify with Google
- Can be the cheapest, smallest office available ($500/month)
- Nobody has to visit this office — it's for Google
- **Service area businesses without visible address have significantly harder time ranking. Non-negotiable.**

### Primary business category
- Search "personal injury lawyer" in your city
- Look at what the top 3 ranked profiles have selected
- Match them EXACTLY
- Example: if top 3 are listed as "personal injury attorney" — pick that, NOT "lawyer"
- **A wrong category can tank your entire campaign.** Switching can double calls in 30 seconds.

### Business description (use all 750 characters)
- Include "personal injury lawyer" + your city name naturally
- Include types of cases: car accidents, truck accidents, slip and falls, wrongful death
- Google reads description to understand what you do + where

### Products section (overlooked by almost every law firm)
- Even though you are a service business, fill out Products
- Each product gets its own description with keywords + locations naturally included
- Examples:
  - Car Accident Attorney in Dallas
  - Truck Accident Lawyer in Dallas
  - Motorcycle Accident Attorney in Dallas
  - Wrongful Death Lawyer in Dallas

## 2. Reviews — THE HIGHEST ROI ACTIVITY

> "Former Google employees have said that reviews in the last 90 days are the #1 ranking factor for the map pack."

### The cold-call program
- Personally CALL every former client you've ever won a case for
- Not texting. Not emailing. Calling.
- Script: "Hey, I hope you're doing well. I'm a small business owner trying to grow my practice. No pressure at all, but it would mean the world to me if you could leave me a Google review."

### The detailed review template
- Write the review yourself if it's a close friend
- Include: photo + long detailed description + keywords ("personal injury," "car accident," city name)
- Target 3-4 sentences minimum from clients
- Cover: their experience + case type + outcome

### Pacing (avoid Google flagging)
- First 10 reviews: 1 per day max
- 10 → 20: 2 per day max
- 20 → 30: 3 per day max
- And so on...

### Reviews to AVOID
- Same last name as another reviewer (often get removed)
- Reviewers from outside your area (e.g. Miami review for Denver office = flag risk)

### Target count
- Identify top 3 ranked profiles in your area
- If they have 200, 150, 100 reviews → target 200 minimum to compete
- If a competitor has exact-match profile name like "Dallas Personal Injury Lawyer" with 50 reviews — exact match names carry weight, plan for SIGNIFICANTLY more

### Response discipline
- Every review responded to within 24 hours
- Include keywords naturally in response
- Example: "Thank you for trusting our personal injury team here in Dallas. We're glad we could help with your car accident case."
- This is freshness — Google wants to see you have a pulse

## 3. Citations

A citation = listing of business name, address, phone number on another website.

### Essential citations for PI lawyers
- Yelp (critical — Perplexity + Apple Maps pull from Yelp)
- BBB
- Avvo
- FindLaw
- Justia
- Super Lawyers
- Lawyers.com
- Martindale
- Nolo
- State bar association directory
- Local chamber of commerce
- **Uber business listing** — novel idea, signals "real business" to Google

### NAP consistency (Name / Address / Phone)
- Accuracy 10× more important than volume
- If phone number is wrong on even ONE → hurts rankings
- Same name, address, phone EVERYWHERE
- This is the critical fundamental

---

## 4. Photos and Updates

### Photos
- Upload REAL photos every single week
- Office. Team. Shaking hands with clients. Court appearances. Community events.
- **NO stock photos. EVER.**
- Click-through rate is a ranking factor — engaging photos compound to higher rankings → more clicks → more rankings

### GBP Posts
- Weekly cadence
- GBP posts don't directly move rankings, but freshness + engagement matter
- Example: "Just secured a $250,000 settlement for a client involved in a rear-end collision here in Fort Worth."
- Builds trust + activity signals

## 5. Website Strategy

### DO NOT write blog posts as the foundation
- **Cannot stress this enough.** If your SEO agency is charging thousands a month for blogs like "what to do after a car accident" — you're wasting money.
- Those rank nationally → reader in Minnesota never becomes Texas client
- **You want BUYERS, not browsers**
- Buyer searches: "personal injury attorney near me"
- Browser searches: "how much do personal injury lawyers cost"

### DO build location pages
- One per city + suburb you want to rank in
- Houston metro example: Katy, Sugar Land, Pasadena, The Woodlands, Pearland, League City, Missouri City, Cypress, Spring, Humble
- Each targets "personal injury lawyer" + city

### URL structure
- ✅ `/personal-injury-lawyer-katy-tx`
- ❌ `/services`
- ❌ `/areas-we-serve`
- Google reads URL to understand page

### H1 tag
- Include keyword + city
- ❌ "Welcome to Our Firm"
- ✅ "Katy's Trusted Personal Injury Law Firm"

### Content
- At least 500 words high-quality UNIQUE content per page
- NOT same copy with city name swapped (Google catches that)

### Meta titles
- Matter more than people think
- ✅ "Personal Injury Lawyer in Katy TX | Firm Name"
- Front-load the keyword

### Phone visibility
- Visible on every page without scrolling
- Don't make people hunt for contact info

## 6. Link Building

> "Backlinks are how Google determines who is popular. It is a popularity contest. Always has been."

### Local backlinks
- Local charities you sponsor
- Local news stations
- Local business associations
- Chamber of commerce
- Local event sponsorships
- **Even if domain rating is low** — if it's a real local business, it helps

### Legal directories
- Avvo, FindLaw, Justia
- Double as citations AND backlinks

### Distribution
- 80-90% of links to homepage (looks most natural)
- Interlink from homepage to location pages

### Anchor text discipline
- **Use BRANDED anchors** for majority: "Smith Law Firm," "Smith Personal Injury"
- Branded = safest, won't get penalized
- ❌ AVOID exact-match like "personal injury lawyer Dallas" repeatedly
- Exact match anchors WILL wreck your site

## 7. The Second GBP play

**One of the most fun things for personal injury attorneys.**

- GBPs have ranking distance limits from the office
- Open a second GBP in different part of metro OR different part of state
- **Essentially doubles coverage**
- PI cases worth 10K-100K+ each
- Process:
  1. Get a cheap office
  2. File an LLC
  3. Put a sign up
  4. Get GBP verified
  5. Run up reviews
  6. Build citations
- These GBPs turn into multi-million-dollar assets

## 8. Ranking in AI / LLMs

Where things are headed: Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity.

### 90% is the same as Google ranking
- Solid content
- High quality links
- Optimized GBP
- Location pages

### Other 10% — get listed where AI pulls from
- Avvo
- Yelp
- Justia
- FindLaw
- State bar directory
- Legal listicles ("best personal injury lawyers in [city]")

### Brand mentions matter
- Don't have to be linked
- Just your firm name appearing on:
  - Reddit
  - User-generated content
  - Authoritative websites
- Increases odds of being recommended by AI platforms

### Google AI Mode already pulls from GBPs
- Reads your reviews
- Gives recommendations based on what customers say
- **Game has shifted from "get as many reviews as possible" to "get high-quality detailed reviews AI can read and reference"**

## 9. The Math (ROI context)

## 10. What NOT to Do

- ❌ Sign up with a mega agency (you get lost in shuffle, 40K/yr fresh-grad runs your account)
- ❌ Pay $20K/mo to big PI SEO firms (same results at fraction of price)
- ❌ Big agencies are website-website-website while everyone with a case goes to map pack first
- ❌ Write blogs as foundation of strategy
- ❌ Ignore your phone (firms double inbound calls then complain "no value" because they don't answer)
- ❌ Rely on one marketing channel (what if GBP gets suspended tomorrow?)
