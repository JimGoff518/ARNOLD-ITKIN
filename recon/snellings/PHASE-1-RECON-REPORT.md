## Strategic recommendations

1. **Snellings is the most beatable suburb incumbent in the dataset.** Goff's Frisco landing page should target this firm specifically with: comprehensive content + schema + reviews + Spanish + mass-tort hook.
2. **Don't fight on volume.** Snellings has 50+ documented results. Match their content depth (50+ FAQ pages, case stories) but don't try to out-volume them on intake.
3. **Leverage the verdict gap.** Once Goff has any verdict above $3.66M, that becomes a direct comparison Snellings cannot match.
4. **Spanish play.** Frisco has growing Hispanic population. Snellings has zero Spanish. Goff Spanish Frisco page = uncontested by them.
5. **Childcare injury vertical.** They claim it but don't appear to dominate. If Goff has any child-injury cases, niche specialty page could outrank them.
