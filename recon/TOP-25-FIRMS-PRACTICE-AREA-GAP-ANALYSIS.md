## What this audit confirms strategically

1. **Goff's 17 current practice areas are competent baseline coverage** — most are standard PI verticals that every competitor has. Solid foundation.

2. **The two practice areas to REMOVE** are Medical Malpractice and Nursing Home Abuse (both currently on site, both on Jim's exclusion list, both economically untenable in Texas for non-specialists).

3. **The biggest growth opportunity is mass torts**, not standard PI verticals. Adding more standard PI pages (e.g. another rideshare page) provides marginal value. Adding 6 new federal MDL pages provides massive uncontested SERP territory + high per-case fee economics.

4. **A few non-mass-tort niches are worth considering** — particularly apartment shooting / negligent security (MR.LAW's pioneered category, only 1/25 has it) and construction zone driver injuries (Daniel Stark only).

5. **Goff's exclusion list is professionally correct.** The 12+ competitors with med mal pages are either specialists (Aldous) who have the experts to take selectively or generalists who lose money on the cases they sign. Don't follow them into this.

6. **Tate Law Offices** is worth studying as a structural model — their practice area menu is the most comprehensive in DFW (80+ named case types). Not all of those are economically reasonable for a solo, but the breadth-of-coverage signals "we can help" to a wide audience.

7. **The Spanish opportunity extends beyond hair relaxer.** Vecchio Injury Law is the only competitor explicitly bilingual outside the major Spanish-language firms (Reyes, Thompson, McCraw, Lyons & Simmons). Spanish-language landing pages for niche mass torts (AFFF Spanish, Paragard Spanish, etc.) are uncontested across the entire DFW competitive landscape.
