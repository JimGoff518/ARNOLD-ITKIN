## Strategic recommendations

1. **Reclassify McCraw out of "suburb specialist" framing.** They're a multi-region North Texas firm, not a single-suburb player. Update scoreboard accordingly.
2. **Re-evaluate the McKinney suburb expansion priority.** McCraw's roots + 6 offices + Spanish + Board Cert credentials make McKinney **the hardest suburb to displace** in the dataset. Consider deprioritizing aggressive McKinney push in favor of:
   - **Allen** (Burress + Hwang) — fewer competitors per recon priority list
   - **Plano** (Vecchio + Mullen) — beatable
   - **Frisco** (Snellings + Mullen) — most beatable
3. **For McKinney itself, focus on:**
   - Mass-tort emerging litigation (uncontested by McCraw)
   - Medical malpractice referrals (uncontested)
   - Galaxy Gas Collin County (uncontested)
4. **The Board Certification gap is real.** Goff should pursue Board Certification in Personal Injury Trial Law if not already pursuing — this is the single most differentiating attorney credential in Texas PI and McCraw uses it well.
5. **Spanish content gap.** McCraw + Reyes + Lyons & Simmons + Thompson all have Spanish. Goff is in growing minority of competitors WITHOUT Spanish.
