## Brand voice — modern, aggressive, social

J. Alexander Law's marketing is the most stylistically distinct in the dataset:

- **Hero H1:** "Standing on Business"
- **Tagline:** "Serious Injuries. Serious Results. No Fees Unless We Recover Money On Your Behalf!"
- **Phone branding:** 1-800-INJURED + (214) 206-3264
- **Domain branding:** **severeinjurylawyers.com** — keyword-exact-match domain (KEM)

### "Standing on Business" is a Gen Z slang phrase

This is a sharp signal of brand-voice differentiation. "Standing on business" is a 2023-2024 cultural moment phrase meaning "doing what you said you'd do, no excuses, taking action." It signals:
- **Target demographic:** Younger client base (25-45), urban, social-media-engaged
- **Brand voice:** Casual, confident, aggressive — opposite of Hamilton Wingo's white-glove or Reyes's volume-credibility tone
- **Marketing sophistication:** They're paying attention to cultural language, not using boilerplate legal copy

### Sports partnerships
- **Dallas Stars** — official partner
- American Airlines Center
- SMU Athletics
- Sporting United Soccer League

This is **modern community-equity marketing** — investing in local Dallas sports culture rather than billboards. Builds trust through cultural adjacency rather than direct advertising.

### Instagram presence
**@jalex_law** — active social account. None of the other reconnoitered firms have prominent modern social presence (most have token Facebook pages).

**Strategic takeaway:** J. Alexander Law is **playing a different game** than the established firms — younger demographic targeting, cultural branding, social-media-first marketing. This makes them a poor head-to-head competitor with Goff (different demographic) but a strong threat for any client under 35 in DFW.
