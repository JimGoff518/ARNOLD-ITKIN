## The reviews moat — 15,000+ five-star Google reviews

This is the **single most defensible asset they have**. 15,000+ Google reviews:
- Is impossible to manufacture in under 3-5 years even with aggressive review-collection programs
- Dominates the "local pack" / GBP signal for every Texas geographic query
- Creates a massive trust signal disadvantage for any new entrant to a Reyes-served market
- Means Reyes Google Business Profile likely outranks competitors in map-pack/3-pack for every PI search in Texas

**For comparison:**
- Birdeye recommends a review-collection program at $300/mo
- A solo PI attorney typically generates 20-40 reviews/year organically
- To reach 15K reviews in 33 years = ~455/year average
- At Goff's current volume, hitting 1,000 reviews would take 5+ years even with aggressive collection

**Implication:** Goff's review-collection program (in Fix-It List Tier 1) is now strategically critical, but cannot close the gap with Reyes in any reasonable time horizon. **Goff should compete on a different signal entirely** — Avvo, niche-specific Best Lawyers, mass-tort verdicts.
