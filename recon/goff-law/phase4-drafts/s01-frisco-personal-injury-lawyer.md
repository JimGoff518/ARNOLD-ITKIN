## Why Frisco Drivers Need an Experienced PI Lawyer

Frisco is one of the fastest-growing cities in the country, and the roads show it. The Dallas North Tollway and Sam Rayburn Tollway (SH 121) carry heavy commuter traffic in and out of Plano, Prosper, Little Elm, and The Colony every morning and evening. US-380 — the east-west spine across north Frisco — is one of the most crash-prone corridors in Collin County. The intersection at FM 423 and US-380 alone produces serious wrecks every month.

Then add the destination traffic: The Star (Cowboys headquarters), Stonebriar Centre, Frisco Square, the PGA Frontier campus on the Plano border, and Toyota Stadium all pull cars in from across DFW. Mix that with construction zones along the Tollway and Eldorado Parkway, and Frisco drivers face crash exposure that a generic "Dallas-area" lawyer often does not understand.

Frisco ISD has more than 60 campuses, which means school-zone wrecks, drop-off-line fender-benders, and teen-driver crashes are common. Median household income in Frisco runs roughly $140,000, which means more two-car households, more daily commuting, and more uninsured/underinsured motorist (UM/UIM) coverage on policies than you would see in other parts of DFW. UM/UIM coverage matters — it is the policy that pays when the other driver does not have enough insurance, and most people do not even realize they carry it.

A Frisco personal injury lawyer who actually knows the city understands these patterns. We do.

## Collin County Court System: What You Need to Know

Frisco sits in Collin County. If your case ends up filed as a lawsuit, it will likely be filed at the Collin County Courthouse at **2100 Bloomdale Road in McKinney**, about 15 to 20 minutes northeast of central Frisco depending on traffic. The Collin County Courthouse handles district court, county court, and family court matters.

A small piece of Frisco crosses into Denton County. If your wreck happened on the Denton County side — west of about Legacy Drive — the case could be filed at the Denton County Courts Complex in Denton. We confirm county at intake.

Why does local court familiarity matter? Because every Texas county has its own pace, its own docket habits, and its own jury pool. Collin County juries tend to be conservative on damages but receptive to clear, well-organized evidence and credible witnesses. Plaintiffs who walk into a Collin County courtroom unprepared get crushed. Plaintiffs who walk in with a clean, documented case do well. A lawyer who has worked in this courthouse knows how the clerks like documents formatted, which judges run tight dockets, and how to schedule mediations without burning months.

Most Frisco wreck victims get treated at one of these facilities:

- **Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Frisco** — on the Centennial Medical Center campus off the Dallas North Tollway. Active ER, surgical services, neurology.

If you or a family member was transferred from a Frisco ER to a downtown Dallas trauma center, that transfer record is one of the most important pieces of evidence in your case. It tells the insurance company the injuries were serious enough that local Frisco resources were not enough. We pull those transfer records early.

If you are being told by your ER doctor to follow up with a specialist and you do not have health insurance — call us. There are ways to get you the imaging, the orthopedic consult, and the physical therapy you need without paying out of pocket. Treatment gaps hurt your case. We help you avoid them.

## Texas Statute of Limitations (Brief Mention)

In Texas, you generally have **two years from the date of the crash** to file a personal injury lawsuit. That deadline comes from Texas Civil Practice and Remedies Code § 16.003. There are a few exceptions — claims against government entities can require notice in as little as 6 months, and claims involving minors can have different rules — but two years is the default.

Do not wait. Even though you have two years to file, evidence disappears in weeks. We have a full breakdown on our [Statute of Limitations FAQ page](/faq/personal-injury-statute-of-limitations-texas/).
