## Things to AVOID across all 16 pages

- **NO competitor names.** Don't mention Mullen, Witherite, Reyes, Snellings, McCraw, Thompson, Aldous, or any other firm. Differentiate by Goff's positives, not by attacking others.
- **NO result promises.** Avoid "we'll get you the most money," "guaranteed settlement," "we never lose." Texas Rule 7.02 sensitivity.
- **NO "best / top-rated / award-winning / premier / elite"** language. Doesn't help SEO and creates compliance exposure.
- **NO excluded case types in CTAs:** medical malpractice, nursing home abuse, dog bite, workers' comp (pure), birth injury, SSDI, defamation. Disclaim them on every page (filter language is encouraged).
- **NO lawyer jargon.** See the banned words list in the Voice Guide.
- **NO stock photo placeholders.** Visual selection happens elsewhere; just write the copy.
