# Goff Law DFW — Intake SOP

**Version 1.0 — 2026-05-17**
**Owner:** Chelsea (COO) operationalizes; Jim approves the SLA promise and signs off on call handoffs
**Audience:** Whoever answers the phone — Chelsea, intake coordinator, after-hours service, virtual receptionist, Jim himself
**Why this exists:** Content + Voice Guide + GBP reviews + competitive recon all feed leads. **Intake is where leads convert or die.** Every $1 of SEO/content/GBP work leaks 50-90% at intake right now — no after-hours coverage, no bilingual front line, no qualifying script, no SLA. This SOP closes that leak.

---

## The Promise (the public commitment)

> **"Real attorney on the phone in under 10 minutes. 24/7. Bilingual."**
> *"Abogado real en menos de 10 minutos. 24/7. Hablamos español."*

This appears on every page (homepage hero, every Phase 1-5 page CTA, every email signature, every billboard if/when one happens, every Google Business Profile post).

**This promise gates everything else.** Don't put it on the site until the infrastructure backs it. Once it's on the site, every miss = a refund-the-trust-deficit moment.

### Why this promise (vs Reyes / Mullen / Witherite)

| Firm | Speed-to-lead promise | Goff undercut |
|---|---|---|
| **Reyes** | 15-min callback ONLY on Case Calculator funnel; 24-hr on cold contact form | Goff says 10 min on EVERY channel |
| **Mullen** | 24-7 phone answered (but routes to call center) | Goff says ATTORNEY answers, not specialist |
| **Witherite** | "Call 1-800-CAR-WRECK" — handled by intake staff | Same |
| **McCraw** | Free virtual consultation | Same |
| **Tier S firms generally** | Specialist or paralegal answers, attorney follow-up next day | Goff says attorney FIRST, no specialist screening |

**Goff's edge:** small team = attorney actually picks up. That's the moat made operational.

## The 3 SLAs that matter

| Metric | Standard | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone pickup time** | <60 seconds, 24/7 | Industry research: 78% of callers who don't reach a human within 60 seconds go to the next lawyer in their Google search. Reyes hits 60-90 sec on most calls (per Phase 2 recon). 60 seconds matches Tier S parity. |
| **Speed to attorney** | <10 minutes from first contact | Reyes does 15 min on warm leads, 24 hr on cold. Undercutting by 5 min = "they actually picked up FAST" testimonial language. 10 min also = realistic for a small team with proper routing. |
| **Bilingual coverage** | 100% — every call, every time | DFW Hispanic working-class ICP. Reyes has 30% Spanish-only clients per his own profile. Anything less than 100% bilingual = surrendering 25-40% of in-lane leads. |

## Bilingual Coverage Model

### Standard: 100% bilingual on every channel

- **Phone:** First responder asks "Habla español o inglés?" before any qualifying question
- **Web chat:** Spanish toggle + Spanish-speaking responder
- **Text/SMS:** Auto-acknowledge in EN + ES; responder identifies preferred language
- **Email/contact form:** Spanish form variant + Spanish auto-reply

### Tactical requirements

- **Chelsea or intake coordinator:** must be Spanish-fluent OR work with warm-transfer protocol that gets a Spanish-speaker on the line within 60 sec
- **Jim's calls** with Spanish-speaking clients: use a 3-way warm-transfer with a Spanish-speaking team member if Jim isn't Spanish-fluent. NEVER use Google Translate or a translation app on a live call — Hispanic clients can tell instantly and trust is destroyed.
- **Goff hiring priority:** next intake hire = bilingual Spanish-English. Non-negotiable. This is the single biggest team-composition lever for the ICP.

### Mexican-Spanish vocabulary on intake

Match the Voice Guide:
- "Lastimado" not "herido" (more colloquial)
- "Aseguranza" not "aseguradora" (DFW Tex-Mex vernacular)
- "Carro" or "auto" (both fine)
- Formal "usted" on intake (respectful register), informal "tú" on follow-up after rapport
- Address immigration status proactively: *"Su estatus migratorio no afecta su derecho a una compensación bajo la ley de Texas. No le preguntamos."*

## The Qualifying Script (4 questions max — Reyes-pattern minimal friction)

### English version (use verbatim)

After greeting + language confirmation:

> **You:** "Thanks for calling Goff Law. My name is [Chelsea/your name]. Are you calling about an injury?"
>
> **Caller:** [yes — situation]
>
> **You:** "I'm sorry that happened. I want to make sure we can help you, so I'm going to ask 4 quick questions, and then I'll get you on the phone with Jim or one of our attorneys. Sound good?"
>
> **Q1: "When did this happen?"** (Captures statute-of-limitations + freshness)
>
> **Q2: "Are you hurt? Did you see a doctor or go to the ER?"** (Captures injury severity + medical record-keeping start)
>
> **Q3: "What happened — was it a wreck, an accident at work, something with a product?"** (Captures case type)
>
> **Q4: "Did anyone else's insurance get involved? Have you talked to any other lawyer?"** (Captures opposing-party status + competitor contact)

That's it. No more questions on the first call.

### Spanish version (Mexican vernacular, use verbatim)

> **Tú:** "Gracias por llamar a Goff Law. Soy [name]. ¿Está llamando por una lesión?"
>
> **Caller:** [sí — situación]
>
> **Tú:** "Lamento mucho lo que pasó. Voy a hacerle 4 preguntas rápidas y después le paso con Jim o uno de nuestros abogados. ¿Está bien?"
>
> **P1: "¿Cuándo pasó esto?"**
>
> **P2: "¿Está usted lastimado? ¿Fue al doctor o a la sala de emergencias?"**
>
> **P3: "¿Qué pasó — fue un accidente de carro, en el trabajo, o algo con un producto?"**
>
> **P4: "¿Hay otra aseguranza involucrada? ¿Ha hablado con otro abogado?"**

Then: *"Espere un momento, por favor. Jim le va a llamar en menos de 10 minutos. ¿Cuál es el mejor número para contactarle?"*

### What NOT to ask on the first call

- Income / employment status (filters poor clients — opposite of ICP fit)
- Immigration status (filters Hispanic clients — opposite of ICP)
- Police report Y/N (technical detail; Jim asks after consult)
- Specific dollar amounts (no fake estimating — see Voice Guide on the case calculator decision)
- Fault percentage (legal question; Jim asks)
- Pre-existing injuries (filters cases that ARE viable — Jim evaluates)
- Any qualifying that creates friction before the attorney call

## Case Type → SLA Matrix (when does Jim get on the phone?)

| Case type | Speed to attorney | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| **Fatal MVA / wrongful death** | **<10 min, any time of day** | Highest case value; family is in crisis; competitors will get there first |
| **Truck / 18-wheeler** | **<10 min, any time of day** | High value; evidence preservation deadline starts now |
| **Mass-tort / MDL (AFFF, Galaxy Gas, hair relaxer, etc.)** | **<60 min same-day** | Federal court timeline; Jim's lane; high-value cases |
| **Serious MVA with injuries** | **<10 min business hours; <60 min after hours** | Standard PI; insurance adjuster will call them tomorrow |
| **Minor MVA, soft-tissue** | **Within 2 hrs business hours; next morning after hours** | Lower-value; can be qualified by intake first |
| **Third-party work injury (NOT WC)** | **<60 min same-day** | Specialty area Jim explicitly handles |
| **Emerging tort (e-scooter, Tesla autopilot, gig driver, DoorDash)** | **<60 min same-day** | Niche area Jim's positioned for; few firms handle |
| **Out-of-lane case (medmal / nursing home / dog bite / pure WC / birth injury)** | **Intake refers to trusted partner attorney within 30 min** | Filter trains intake; "we'll refer you to someone we trust" maintains brand trust |

### Jim's phone protocol

- Phone on, ringer audible, 24/7
- Smith.ai (or chosen service) has explicit "warm-transfer to Jim for [list]" rules
- If Jim is in trial / oral argument / unavailable: backup attorney designated; intake informed
- Voicemail greeting (Jim's cell): *"This is Jim Goff. If you're calling about an injury, hang up and call (972) 928-0085 — my team will get me on the phone with you in under 10 minutes, 24/7. If this is anyone else, leave a message."*

| Channel | Tagging method |
|---|---|

## Voice Guide on the Phone (the call IS the brand)

Whoever picks up represents the brand. Voice Guide rules apply:

| Don't say (lawyer-speak) | Do say (Voice Guide) |
|---|---|
| "Goff Law, PLLC, this is [name], how may I assist you?" | "Thanks for calling Goff Law. This is [name]. Are you calling about an injury?" |
| "Let me transfer you to one of our intake specialists" | "Hold on, let me get Jim on the phone with you" |
| "I'll need some information about your accident" | "Tell me what happened" |
| "We offer complimentary consultations" | "It's free to call. It's free to hire us. We only get paid if we win" |
| "I'm sorry, that's not the type of case we handle" | "We don't do that kind of case ourselves, but I know who does. Let me get you their number" |
| "Please hold while I transfer you" | "Stay on, I'm getting Jim on now" |
| "Our attorney will return your call within 24 hours" | "Jim's going to call you back in under 10 minutes" |

**Critical rule:** never let the caller hang up without one of three outcomes:
1. Jim on the phone (or scheduled within 10 min)
2. Referral to another firm (with name + number, not a generic suggestion)
3. Clear next-step explanation (Spanish or English) of when they'll hear back
