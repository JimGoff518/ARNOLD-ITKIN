## Strategic synthesis

Goff Law's content profile has a **unique split personality**:
- ✅ **Ahead of competitors** in emerging-litigation niches (Galaxy Gas, Amazon truck, electric scooter, AI liability, Suboxone, hair relaxers, Tepezza, AFFF, sexual assault civil claims).
- ⚠️ **Behind competitors** in core DFW geographic coverage (zero pages in 32 DFW cities including all the wealthy north suburbs and 1M-population Fort Worth).
- ⚠️ **Behind competitors** in standard Texas-PI verticals (workers' comp, oilfield, construction, defective products) that Thompson Law uses to claim its $1.9B settlement total.

**Strategic recommendation:** Don't try to match Thompson Law's surface area. Instead:
1. **Defend + extend the emerging-litigation moat** (mass tort + Galaxy Gas + scooter + Amazon).
2. **Fill the suburb gap** (Frisco/Plano/Allen/McKinney → then Fort Worth/Arlington/Irving).
3. **Add 1-2 missing Texas verticals** (Workers' Comp first — every Texas worker is a potential claim).
4. **Stop spreading content thin** — Houston + East Texas have 25 pages each but they're under 1,500 words. Either deepen them or deprioritize them for DFW focus.
