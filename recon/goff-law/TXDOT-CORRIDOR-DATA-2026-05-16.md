# TxDOT Crash Data for DFW Interstate Corridors

**Purpose:** Source-cited crash statistics for Goff Law DFW highway/truck landing pages.
**Compiled:** 2026-05-16
**Source authority:** Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) — Crash Records Information System (CRIS) + annual Texas Motor Vehicle Traffic Crash Facts reports. All numbers below are TxDOT-published as of the April 9, 2025 data-extract cutoff stated by TxDOT.

---

## CRITICAL METHODOLOGY NOTE — READ FIRST

TxDOT publishes free, public, downloadable PDFs that break crashes down by **county** and by **road type** (Interstate / US & State Highway / Farm-to-Market / County Road / City Street / Tollway / Other). They do NOT publish, in free PDF form, fatal crashes **filtered to a specific named Interstate** (e.g., "I-35E only" or "I-635 only"). That granularity exists inside the CRIS Query Tool (https://cris.dot.state.tx.us/public/Query/app/home) but requires an interactive session and CSV export per query.

**As a result:**
- Every "Interstate" number for Dallas County or Tarrant County in this document is the SUM of all Interstates running through that county. In Dallas County, that's I-20 + I-30 + I-35E + I-45 + I-635 + I-345. In Tarrant County, that's I-20 + I-30 + I-35W + I-820.
- For corridor-specific numbers (I-35E only, etc.), see the **"Data unavailable as of 2026-05-16"** call-outs in each corridor section.
- Honest framing on landing pages: "Across all Dallas County interstates, X fatal crashes; on Tarrant County interstates, Y." Do NOT claim a number is for a single corridor unless it actually is.

**Geographic-overlap warning:** I-20 and I-30 both cross the Dallas/Tarrant county line. Adding "Dallas County I-20" + "Tarrant County I-20" gives a fuller corridor view but double-counts nothing — they are mutually exclusive county segments.

## I-20 through Dallas County + Tarrant County

**Summary paragraph (safe to cite on a landing page):**
I-20 runs east–west across the entire DFW metro, passing through both Tarrant County (south Fort Worth/Arlington) and Dallas County (south Dallas / Mesquite). TxDOT's published statistics show that across all Tarrant County interstates, there were 50 fatal crashes (59 fatalities) in 2024, and across all Dallas County interstates, 81 fatal crashes (90 fatalities) — combined, Dallas + Tarrant County interstates accounted for 131 fatal crashes and 149 deaths in 2024 alone, or roughly one Interstate fatal crash every 67 hours across the two counties. ## Data Limitations

1. **No corridor-isolation in free TxDOT data.** TxDOT's free annual PDFs aggregate by county and road type (Interstate vs US Hwy vs FM, etc.) but do NOT publish a free, downloadable list of "fatal crashes on I-35E in 2024." That level of detail requires an interactive query session inside the TxDOT CRIS Query Tool (https://cris.dot.state.tx.us/public/Query/app/home) and export of CSV results. If the landing pages need true per-corridor numbers, schedule a 1–2 hour CRIS Query session with someone trained on the tool.

2. **Reporting cutoff = April 9, 2025.** All 2024 numbers in this report reflect crash reports processed as of that cutoff. Late-filing CR-3 reports may shift 2024 numbers upward by an estimated 1–3% over the next 12 months. Statewide 2024 fatality total of 4,150 is described by TxDOT as provisional.

3. **CMV ≠ semi-truck only.** TxDOT's Commercial Motor Vehicle category includes any vehicle meeting federal CMV criteria (GVWR > 10,000 lbs, etc.), which is broader than just 18-wheelers/tractor-trailers. If a landing page implies "tractor-trailer" specifically, the CMV number is an OVERSTATEMENT for that vehicle class. To narrow to tractor-trailers only, see TxDOT Table 28 (Vehicles in Crashes by Body Style) or run a CRIS query filtered on body style.

4. **County boundaries cross corridors.** I-20, I-30, and I-35 all cross the Dallas/Tarrant county line. County-segmented Interstate fatal data does NOT give a corridor-end-to-end count. To get a full I-20 picture from Weatherford to Terrell, you would need to combine Parker + Tarrant + Dallas + Kaufman county Interstate data (Parker County 2024 Interstate fatals: 10, fatalities 11; Kaufman County 2024 Interstate fatals: 1, fatalities 1). Per-corridor numbers without this combining will under-represent the full corridor.

5. **"Crash sub-type" (rear-end, jackknife, sideswipe) is not in the county/road-type PDFs.** TxDOT publishes "First Harmful Event" data statewide (Table 19) but not joined to county+road-type. To get rear-end vs jackknife counts on I-635 specifically, that's a custom CRIS query.

6. **Mile-marker hotspot data is not in the standard PDFs.** TxDOT and the Center for Transportation Research at UT Austin produce crash-cluster maps but those are interactive dashboards, not citable static numbers. For a landing page, the safe play is to cite county-level Interstate totals and link to the TxDOT crash analysis dashboard at https://data.texas.gov/stories/s/Texas-Department-of-Transportation-Traffic-Safety-/nze5-dppu/.

7. **CRIS Query Tool is publicly accessible.** Per TxDOT's documentation, the CRIS Query Tool at https://cris.dot.state.tx.us/public/Query/app/home is free, supports filtering by county and by route, retains the prior 10 calendar years plus current, and exports to CSV. If higher granularity is needed for landing pages, run those queries directly.

## How to Cite

**Recommended attribution for use on Goff Law DFW landing pages:**

> Crash data sourced from the Texas Department of Transportation, Texas Motor Vehicle Traffic Crash Facts calendar year 2024 (and corresponding 2022, 2023 annual reports). Statistics reflect reportable crashes from Texas Peace Officer's Crash Reports (CR-3) processed by TxDOT as of April 9, 2025. Full reports available at https://www.txdot.gov/data-maps/crash-reports-records/motor-vehicle-crash-statistics.html. Pulled 2026-05-16.

**Short-form footnote attribution:**

> Source: Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT), 2024 Crash Statistics, Table [12/13/30]. https://www.txdot.gov/data-maps/crash-reports-records/

**Per-statistic citation format (for inline use):**

> [Number] [metric] in [year] (TxDOT 2024 Texas Motor Vehicle Traffic Crash Facts, Table 12)

**DO NOT** cite:
- Wikipedia, law-firm marketing blogs, or trucking-industry news roundups
- Aggregated "interesting facts about I-35" sites
- AAA / IIHS data without verifying against TxDOT (those use FARS, which has different counts than CRIS)
- Any source that doesn't explicitly link to a .txdot.gov URL

**Liability hedge for landing pages:**
> Crash statistics may include incidents not directly relevant to the legal services described and are presented for informational purposes only. Reporting cutoffs and methodology may cause numbers to vary from other published sources.
