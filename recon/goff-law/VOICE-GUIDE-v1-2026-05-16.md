# Goff Law DFW — Voice & Brand Guide

**Version 1.0 — 2026-05-16**
**Owner:** Jim Goff
**Use:** Tape this above the desk of anyone writing for Goff Law — homepage copy, intake scripts, ads, social posts, email signatures, billboards, the 44 drafted content pages, anything that goes out the door under the Goff Law name.

---

## How to use this guide

- Every piece of copy gets pressure-tested against the 10 rules + word list before it ships.
- If it sounds like a corporate law firm wrote it, rewrite it.
- If it sounds like a working person from DFW wrote it (one who happens to be a damn good lawyer), it passes.
- When in doubt: shorter, blunter, more named, more anti-insurance, more bilingual.

## Who we're writing for (the persona)

A 34-year-old single mom in Pleasant Grove. Rear-ended on I-45 by a guy who ran a red. Drives a 2014 Altima. Two kids, works at the Walmart on Buckner. Already missed two days of work. The insurance adjuster called this morning and offered $1,800. She has no lawyers in her family. Her cousin said "you should sue." She's on her phone tonight, on the couch, while her kids sleep. She's scrolling and she's scared.

What stops her scroll: a face she trusts + a phrase she can text her cousin + the feeling that this lawyer isn't going to make her feel small.

What she is NOT thinking about: trial experience, firm size, federal court, board certifications, which judge knows which lawyer, decades of practice, law school pedigree.

What she IS thinking about: *Will this person actually fight for me. Will it cost me anything. Will I be treated like a person.*

Every word we write is for her. If a 14-year-old kid couldn't follow it, rewrite it.

## The 10 voice rules

| # | Rule | DON'T write this | DO write this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Talk like a person, not a lawyer.** | "Our firm pursues maximum compensation for victims of negligence." | "We get you paid when someone hurts you." |
| 2 | **"You" not "the client."** | "If a client is injured in a collision..." | "If you got hurt in a wreck..." |
| 3 | **"We" not "the firm."** | "The firm has handled thousands of cases." | "We've gotten paid for thousands of people." |
| 4 | **Name Jim. Always.** | "the attorney" / "our managing partner" / "your assigned counsel" | "Jim Goff" / "Jim" |
| 5 | **Short sentences. Period periods.** | One 35-word sentence with three subordinate clauses and a parenthetical. | "Jim takes your call. Jim works your case. Jim gets you paid." |
| 6 | **Insurance is the bad guy. Frame it that way.** | "insurance carrier" / "the defendant's insurer" / "opposing party's coverage" | "the insurance company" / "their insurance" — always framed as the opponent, never neutral |
| 7 | **Money words: clear, not coded.** | "compensation," "damages," "recovery," "remuneration" | "money," "paid," "what you're owed" |
| 8 | **No promises of result. Period.** | "We'll get you the most money possible." / "We win every case." | "We fight to get you every dollar we can." / "We don't get paid unless you do." |
| 9 | **Spanish is a feature, not an accommodation.** | "Translation available." / "Spanish interpretation upon request." | "Hablamos español. Mexican-Spanish-speaking team." Lead with it. |
| 10 | **Specifics over adjectives.** | "experienced, top-rated, aggressive, trusted, premier, elite" | Real numbers, real years, real types of cases. If you can't name a number, cut the adjective. |

| Aggressive representation | We fight |
| Tireless advocacy | We pick up the phone |
| Time of need | When you need help |
| Insurance carrier | The insurance company |
| Opposing party | The person who hurt you |
| Counsel | Lawyer |
| Retain our services | Hire us / call us |
| Schedule a consultation | Call Jim / call us |
| Subsequent to the accident | After the wreck |
| At your earliest convenience | When you can |
| Should you have any questions | If you have questions |
| Premier / elite / top-rated | (just cut — say what you actually do) |

**One-line test:** If the word would make the Pleasant Grove mom say "what does that mean?" — rewrite it.

## Tone calibrations by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| **Intake phone call** | Warm, urgent, direct. *"What happened? Are you okay? OK, here's what we do next."* Listen first. |
| **Homepage** | Bold, simple, name + outcome + family. Hook above the fold. |
| **Phase 1 FAQ pages** | Plain-English teacher voice. Explain like to a smart adult who's never used a lawyer. No condescension. |
| **Phase 2 truck pages** | Add TxDOT data for trust. Keep voice plain. Insurance/trucking company = the opponent. |
| **Phase 3 mass-tort pages** | Add MDL court + science (NIH study, FDA action) for authority. Voice stays plain. |
| **Phase 4 location pages** | Local landmarks. Named neighborhoods. *"If you got hurt on I-30 in Mesquite..."* Show you actually know the area. |
| **Wrongful death intake (any channel)** | Quiet. Reverent. **No slogans on this page.** Just: *"We are so sorry for your loss. When you're ready, we'll be here. Jim takes these calls personally."* |
| **Spanish content** | Mexican vernacular (carro, aseguranza, "la familia"). Warmer. More family-anchored. Address immigration concerns directly: *"Su estatus no importa. No preguntamos."* |
| **Ads (radio / TV / digital / billboard)** | Pure hook + CTA. No fluff. No legal disclaimers visible in the hook itself. |
| **Social media** | Jim's voice, not corporate. Photo of Jim or a real client (with permission). One thought per post. |
| **Email signature** | Name. One line. Phone. Done. *Jim Goff · Goff Law DFW · (XXX) XXX-XXXX · We pick up the phone.* |

## Visual voice (because brand is more than words)

- **Jim's actual face on every page that allows it.** Real photos. Not stock. Not in a conference room with leather books. Not against a Dallas skyline. Outside in a normal shirt is better than inside in a suit.
- **Photos of real DFW neighborhoods** — Mesquite parking lots, South Oak Cliff streets, Garland intersections. Not Dallas-downtown skyline (says "downtown lawyer who doesn't live here").
- **Real client photos with written permission and proper compensation.** No models playing victims. No stock photos of people in neck braces.
- **Use a face for the human story; use numbers for the case story.** Don't substitute one for the other.

### Banned imagery (never use, anywhere)

- Scales of justice
- Gavels
- Lady Justice with the blindfold
- Leather law books in a stack
- Lawyers in suits in a conference room around a table
- Stock photos of "diverse business team"
- Generic American flag in the background of a portrait
- Courthouse columns in soft focus
- Handshakes in suits
- Briefcases

### Preferred imagery

- Jim, real-photo, looking at the camera, somewhere recognizable in DFW
- Hand-held shots from real intersections / hospital parking lots
- Real Spanish-language signage in the background where appropriate
- Working-class DFW context (gas stations, taquerias, Walmarts, schools — not luxury hotels or art museums)

## Quick before-and-after examples

### Example 1 — Homepage hero
**BEFORE:**
> Goff Law is a premier personal injury law firm dedicated to providing aggressive representation and maximum compensation for victims of negligence throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex.

**AFTER:**
> # Get Goff. Get Paid.
> *You're not a case. You're family.*
> Hurt in a wreck? Jim Goff fights the insurance company so you don't have to.
> **No win. No fee.** · Hablamos español. · We pick up the phone.
> **Call Jim now: (XXX) XXX-XXXX**

### Example 2 — About-page lead
**BEFORE:**
> Jim Goff is a seasoned personal injury attorney with extensive experience advocating for clients throughout North Texas in matters arising from motor vehicle collisions, workplace accidents, and other instances of negligence.

**AFTER:**
> Jim Goff is a Dallas personal injury lawyer who fights for working people in DFW. He started Goff Law in 2023. He takes the calls himself. He works the cases himself. He gets paid when you get paid.

### Example 3 — Intake-call close (script)
**BEFORE:**
> Thank you for contacting our office. An attorney will reach out to you shortly to schedule a complimentary consultation regarding your potential claim.

**AFTER:**
> "Thanks for calling. Jim's going to call you back in the next hour. He'll listen to what happened. He'll tell you straight up if you have a case. If you do, we get to work today. You pay nothing unless we win. Okay?"

### Example 4 — Phase 4 location-page lead
**BEFORE:**
> Goff Law proudly serves the residents of Mesquite, Texas, providing comprehensive personal injury legal services for victims of motor vehicle collisions and other negligence-based incidents.

**AFTER:**
> If you got hurt in a wreck in Mesquite — on US-80, in a Town East parking lot, anywhere in 75149 or 75150 — you can call Jim Goff. He's a Dallas lawyer who fights for Mesquite families. No fee unless we win. Hablamos español.

## Compliance note (one-time read)

The voice in this guide intentionally avoids result-promises ("we'll get you the most"), guarantee language ("we always win"), and specific-amount claims unless backed by a real, compliant case result. Jim handles Texas Disciplinary Rules compliance — every writer using this guide should still avoid those traps in first drafts.
