The Strike List

Ten moves, ranked by ROI. Mix of immediate wins and longer plays. Every move has a documented basis in the recon artifacts.

1
Implement schema markup
QUICK WIN · ~8h dev
Attorney + AggregateRating + Service + Offer + FAQPage on every page. Thompson Law and most competitors haven't. SERP rich-result wins.
2
Fix the homepage hero
QUICK WIN · ~1 day dev
Replace empty "MILLIONS WON" hero with: specific case result + trust badges + lead form + review count. Spec ready at recon/goff-law/HERO-FIX-DRAFT.md.
3
Build dedicated Frisco / Plano / Allen / McKinney English pages
HIGH ROI
Wealthy DFW suburbs. Mullen has Plano + Frisco offices but lacks Allen / McKinney depth. Thompson Law has Spanish-only or thin English.
4
Reframe positioning: "complex cases" not "local"
STRATEGIC
Mullen owns "local family firm" — Goff's "lifelong Dallas resident" angle isn't unique. Goff should own "trial-ready insurance defense alum for complex cases."
5
Display Goff's actual review count + rating
QUICK WIN · ~1h
Mullen leads with 567/4.9★. Goff hides theirs. Whatever Goff has — show it. Wire a Google Reviews widget today.
6
Double down on mass tort + emerging litigation
DEFEND & EXTEND
Mullen has 0 mass tort coverage. Thompson has 0. Goff has AFFF, Tepezza, PFAS, AI liability, hair relaxers, Galaxy Gas. Build the cluster, dominate the niche.
7
SMS opt-in + same-minute auto-response
CONVERSION LIFT
Witherite captures SMS consent at form submit. Goff doesn't. Add TCPA-compliant opt-in checkbox + 60-second auto SMS response. Faster intake = higher close rate.
8
Re-pull Ahrefs/SEMrush against corrected URLs
OPS
All previous SEO metrics in the strategy docs were measured against wrong-URL Thompson Law. Re-pull DA / referring domains / organic keywords against 1800lionlaw.com, mullenandmullen.com, witheritelaw.com.
9
Run Phase 2 deep-dive on Mullen & Mullen
RECON
Phase 1 done. Schema audit, backlink profile, blog discovery, lead-form mystery shop, pricing math audit. They're the real organic threat.
10
Spanish-language coverage
LONG GAME
Thompson Law has 150+ Spanish PI pages. Goff has zero. ~30% of DFW PI claimants are Hispanic. Multi-six-figure revenue opportunity.
