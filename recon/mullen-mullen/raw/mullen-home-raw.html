Enjoy an Easy Process

Case Evaluation
If you were hurt in an accident, it is critical to speak with an experienced attorney about the specific circumstances of your potential personal injury case, including whether a person or company was negligent in causing your injuries, the extent and severity of your injuries, medical treatment, and your legal options. Our team of Dallas injury lawyers provides free consultations and case evaluations by phone, video call, or in-person. There’s never a fee for an initial consultation and – if you retain us – there’s never any fees unless we win your case.
01
Investigation and Case Development
After you retain us, we get to work right away investigating all aspects of your claim. This includes requesting the police report and associated documents from law enforcement, obtaining any necessary witness statements, and retaining experts if needed to win your case. Our firm will assist you in obtaining any necessary medical treatment, handle all bills and records requests, and communicate with applicable insurance companies. Our job is to make your life easier! You can text your dedicated legal team directly 24/7 as your case develops.
02
Strategy
Every case is unique, which means each strategy is also unique and highly customized. Sometimes we may file a personal injury claim right away, other times we may wait until medical treatment concludes. In each situation, our team uses our cutting-edge technology, advanced resources, and extensive experience to plan every step of a client’s case strategy.
03
Demand Letter
A settlement demand is a detailed letter that outlines your case, including liability and damages (injuries, medical bills, lost wages, pain & suffering, mental anguish, etc.). The letter is typically sent to the insurance company, the at-fault party, or both. They will either reject the demand, make a counteroffer, or accept the demand. Putting together a strong, persuasive demand package is critical to a successful, timely result.
04
Case Settles or Lawsuit is Filed
Most Mullen & Mullen claims settle during the demand negotiation process. We are committed to obtaining the best possible result for our clients, however, and never hesitate to recommend litigation if it is in YOUR best interest. It is important to work with an experienced personal injury lawyer who will fight for you no matter what it takes.
