1. H1: "Dallas [Practice Area] Attorneys"
2. Hero claim: "$10 billion in settlements and verdicts" + "200+ years of collective experience"
3. Phone CTA: (214) 234-7911 + "Se habla español" + "calls 24/7" + "virtual meetings available"
4. Long-form copy (2,800-3,200 words per page)
5. **FAQ block** (with statute of limitations, compensation types, claim-strength criteria)
6. **Featured case results** (sliding carousel: $7.3B → $32M brain injury range)
7. **Video testimonials**
8. **Internal linking** to other practice areas
9. **Schema markup** (JSON-LD blocks for Organization, LegalService, Reviews per WebFetch indication)
10. Contact form: "Let's Get Started on Your Case Today"

**This same template runs across 47+ practice-area pages** — every PI vertical (car, truck, motorcycle, slip-fall, rideshare, drunk driving, premises liability, dog bite-equivalent), every catastrophic vertical (industrial, oilfield, construction, scaffolding, welding, crane, electrocution, fires/explosions), and complex litigation areas.
