## Content Types & Distribution

### Monthly Content Quotas

| Content Type | Quantity | Word Count | Purpose |
|-------------|----------|------------|---------|
| Practice Area Pages | 1-2 | 2,000-3,000 | Core SEO, conversions |
| Blog Posts | 4-6 | 1,200-2,000 | Keyword expansion, authority |
| FAQ Sections | 1 | 500-1,000 | Schema, featured snippets |
| Case Studies | 1 | 800-1,200 | Social proof, conversions |
| Video Scripts | 1-2 | 500-800 | YouTube, social media |

### Content Optimization Checklist

For every piece of content:
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
- [ ] LSI keywords naturally distributed
- [ ] Internal links to 3-5 related pages
- [ ] External links to 1-2 authoritative sources
- [ ] FAQ schema where applicable
- [ ] Local schema (Dallas, DFW, Texas)
- [ ] Clear CTA above fold and end of content
- [ ] Mobile-optimized formatting
- [ ] Meta description with keyword + value prop
- [ ] Alt text on all images
