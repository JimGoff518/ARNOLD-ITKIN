## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive paid media strategy designed to:
- Generate immediate leads while SEO builds momentum
- Target high-intent keywords with strong conversion potential
- Maximize ROI through smart budget allocation and optimization
- Scale strategically based on performance data

## Local Service Ads (LSA)

**Priority: CRITICAL**
**Budget: $3,000-5,000/month**

### Why LSA is Essential
- Pay per lead (not per click)
- "Google Guaranteed" badge
- Top of search results
- Phone calls directly to your firm
- Higher trust factor

### Setup Requirements
1. Complete Google Business Profile verification
2. Background check completion
3. License verification
4. Insurance verification
5. Reviews collection (minimum 10-15)

### Categories to Enable
- Personal Injury Lawyer
- Car Accident Lawyer
- Truck Accident Lawyer
- Wrongful Death Lawyer

### Budget Optimization
- Set weekly budget caps
- Geographic radius: 50-75 miles from Dallas
- Business hours focus (with after-hours option)
- Target cost per lead: $150-250

## Conversion Tracking Setup

### Required Tracking

| Conversion Type | Value | Tracking Method |
|----------------|-------|-----------------|
| Phone Call (30+ sec) | $300 | CallRail + Google Ads |
| Form Submission | $200 | Google Tag Manager |
| Live Chat Initiation | $50 | GTM Event |
| Click-to-Call (Mobile) | $100 | GTM Event |

### Call Tracking Setup
1. Install CallRail or similar
2. Create tracking numbers for each campaign
3. Set up dynamic number insertion (DNI)
4. Configure call recording (with consent)
5. Set minimum call duration for conversion (30 seconds)

### Form Tracking
1. Track form submissions as conversions
2. Pass conversion data to Google Ads
3. Set up enhanced conversions
4. Track by campaign source

## A/B Testing Roadmap

### Month 1-2: Ad Copy Tests
- Headlines: Benefit vs. Feature focused
- Descriptions: Urgency vs. Trust focused
- CTAs: "Call Now" vs. "Get Free Consultation"

### Month 3-4: Landing Page Tests
- Form length: Short (3 fields) vs. Medium (5 fields)
- Above-fold: Form vs. Phone number prominent
- Social proof: Video testimonials vs. Written reviews

### Month 5-6: Audience Tests
- In-market audiences
- Custom intent audiences
- Demographic targeting

### Ongoing
- New headline variations monthly
- Landing page iteration based on heatmaps
- Bid strategy testing
